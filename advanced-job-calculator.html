How this works: For Fixed items, enter the annual total. For Per Van or Per Person items, enter the cost FOR ONE van or ONE person — the calculator multiplies by your current count.

Example: If one van costs $9,000/year in fuel, type 9000 and select "Per Van". With 4 vans, total = $36,000. With 5 vans, $45,000.

Type guide: Fixed — same regardless of vans/staff. Per Van — multiplied by van count. Per Person — multiplied by team count.
